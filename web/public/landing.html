A lantern for the trenches.

Vigil is a Chrome extension. Click the small V next to any Solana token: it reads the launch and stamps it Clean, Watch or Bundled, with the reason in one sentence.

Add to Chrome

Three seals. Nothing in between.

No score to decode. Vigil stamps each token with one of three words and a mark you learn in a second, and tells you the fact behind it.

Clean

One dot, alone. The launch looks spread out: no wallet in control, no dev with a rug history.

Watch

A dot under watch. Nothing proves a rug, but something is off. Look twice before you buy.

Bundled

Three dots, one player. Wallets that bought together, or a dev who has done this many times. Stay out.

Click the small V after any token's name: a few seconds later, it turns into one of these seals.

how it works

The night shift.

22:00

You open your terminal.

A small V sits after every token's name. Nothing runs until you choose.

22:01

You click the one you want.

Vigil reads that launch only: the first ten seconds of buys, the dev's past launches, who funded the first wallets.

22:02

The seal lands.

In a few seconds, right next to the name. The panel opens with the numbers and the exit radar.

Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc and Opera. The Chrome Web Store listing is on its way; until then it installs in four steps.

Free on the Chrome Web Store, checked by Google. Works in Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc and Opera.

  1. Open Vigil on the Chrome Web StoreThe button on the left. Checked and installed by Google.
  2. Click Add to ChromeChrome shows what Vigil reads: token addresses on the sites it supports.
  3. Pin the VPuzzle icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to Vigil.
  4. Open your terminalClick the V after any token's name. The seal lands in seconds.
