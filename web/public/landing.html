A lantern for the trenches.

Vigil is a Chrome extension. Every Solana token you scroll past gets lit up and stamped Clean, Watch or Bundled, with the reason in one sentence.

Add to Chrome

Three seals. Nothing in between.

No score to decode. Vigil stamps each token with one of three words, and tells you the fact behind it.

Clean

The launch looks spread out. No wallet in control, no dev with a rug history.

Watch

Nothing proves a rug, but something is off. Look twice before you buy.

Bundled

One player took the launch, or the dev has done this many times. Stay out.
