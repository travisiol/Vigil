Three verdicts. One reason each.

Pools, bonding curves and protocol wallets are left out, so only real wallets count. Every verdict names the fact that decided it.

Bundled

Stay out.

One player took the launch, or the dev has done this many times.

  • 30% or more of the supply bought in the first 10 seconds
  • One wallet holding 20% or more
  • Wallets funded by the same source holding a large share
  • A serial dev (20+ launches) or a dev farm
Watch

Look twice.

Nothing proves a rug, but something isn't right.

  • 15% or more taken in the first seconds
  • Mint or freeze authority still active
  • Liquidity under 5% of market cap
  • A dev with 5 or more past launches
Clean

Nothing found.

None of the warning signs. A fact about the launch, not a promise.

  • A spread-out launch, no early grab
  • No single wallet in control
  • No funding links between top holders
  • A clean token can still go to zero

first 10 seconds

A real crowd can't buy that fast; a bundle can. Every early buy is read and sniper bots are flagged.

funding links

Where each early wallet's SOL came from, two hops back. Ten wallets funded by one source are one player.

dev history

How many tokens the creator launched before, and whether its wallet was made minutes before launch.

holders

The biggest wallet and the top 10, plus wallets labelled bundler, insider or rat trader.

authorities and liquidity

Whether someone can still mint or freeze, and how much real money sits behind the price.

exit radar

What the launch wallets still hold right now, and whether they are selling together.

Read-only. And you can check it.

An extension sees the pages you visit, so you should know exactly what this one does.

What Vigil does

  • Reads token addresses on the supported sites, nowhere else.
  • Sends those addresses to Vigil's server, which reads the chain and answers with a verdict.
  • Remembers one setting on your computer.

What Vigil never does

  • Never touches a wallet. No connection, no signature.
  • Never reads your history, your cookies or any other site.
  • Never asks for an account on the free plan.

The code is public

A few small files you can read before installing. The only permission is storage, plus access to the supported sites. Privacy policy.

Questions

Facts, not financial advice. Vigil shows what happened on-chain; the decision stays yours.

What is Vigil?

A browser extension that detects rugs on Solana memecoins. It doesn't trade and doesn't replace your terminal: it sits on top of the sites you already use and puts a Clean, Watch or Bundled verdict next to every token.

Which sites does it work on?

Axiom, GMGN, pump.fun, Terminal (Padre), DexScreener and X. Photon and BullX are in beta.

How fast is a verdict?

A few seconds for a token nobody has checked yet; on pump.fun we measured 30 brand-new tokens read in about 45 seconds. After that it's instant, because verdicts are shared.

Does it see my wallet?

No. It never connects to a wallet and never signs anything. The only thing it sends is the token addresses on your screen. Read the privacy policy.

Is it free?

The Free plan is, for good. Pro costs $29 a month, paid in SOL, or you unlock it by holding at least $20 of $VIGIL. Under $20 of $VIGIL, you stay on the Free plan.

Is a Clean verdict a buy signal?

No. A verdict is a fact about how the token launched and who holds it, not a prediction. A clean token can still go to zero.

What is $VIGIL?

The community token behind the project. You don't need it for the Free plan. To unlock Pro with it, your wallet must hold at least $20 worth of $VIGIL.
